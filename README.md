## Projects

### [Green Transition Dashboard](https://github.com/AbdulZeeshan688/Green-Transition-dashboard)

Dashboard for an electric vehicle digital twin. You can drive the car, and it shows battery, range, energy use and CO₂ live, compares routes by energy, and switches between six real EV models. I was group lead in a team of four, for LAB REDI 2026 (part of the EMDT project by LAB and LUT University).

`React` `Vite` `Chart.js` `FastAPI` `pytest`

### [Kimbtron LinkedIn Publisher](https://github.com/AbdulZeeshan688/kimbtron-linkedin-publisher) · [live demo](https://silver-bombolone-13f32f.netlify.app)

Built during my internship. Log in with LinkedIn, generate a post with AI, edit it and publish it from one page. Perplexity does the research, Gemini writes the draft, drafts are saved in Notion.

`n8n` `JavaScript` `LinkedIn OAuth 2.0` `Gemini API` `Notion API` `Netlify`

### [DataLens](https://github.com/AbdulZeeshan688/DataLens-AI-Powered-Data-Analytics-Platform)

Upload a CSV or Excel file and it profiles the data (missing values, duplicates, column types, revenue trends), then uses OpenAI to write short business insights about it. Still in progress.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `pandas` `OpenAI API` `React` `TypeScript` `Docker`

### [RERUN](https://github.com/AbdulZeeshan688/rerun)

A time-loop mystery game played in the terminal. The town floods at 14:00 every loop, and the only thing you keep is your notebook of facts. Pure Python, no dependencies, with tests.

`Python` `pytest`
